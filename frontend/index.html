[ STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES // FAQ ]

FREQUENTLY ASKED PROTOCOLS

HOW DOES THE SYSTEM PROCESS IMAGES WITH STRIPPED EXIF GPS METADATA? +

When images are uploaded via platforms that strip EXIF tags (such as WhatsApp or Telegram), the backend activates its automated Odisha geo-bounding fallback engine. The image content is hashed and matched against known vulnerability hotspots in Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, and Puri, with subtle micro-jitter to emulate street-level precision while maintaining location realism.

WHAT ROLE DOES GEMMA 4 PLAY IN THE INCIDENT EVALUATION CYCLE? +

Gemma 4 (`gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`) serves as the core vision reasoning backbone. Stationed under the SEOC system prompt, it assesses primary structural or civic failures, detects imminent life safety threats (such as live electricity in contact with floodwater), assigns one of four strict severity ratings, and designates the precise tactical responders required.

CAN THIS BACKEND STREAM LIVE GEOJSON TO EXTERNAL GIS DASHBOARDS? +

Yes. All validated incidents are immediately published to the `/api/incidents/geojson` endpoint in RFC 7946 FeatureCollection format. This feed plugs directly into Leaflet, Mapbox, Deck.gl, or emergency control room GIS displays.

HOW IS DATA PERSISTED ACROSS SEOC SERVER RESTARTS? +

The backend utilizes a thread-safe in-memory cache synchronized with `data/incidents.json`. On initial boot, it loads persistent incident files or initializes with realistic coastal Odisha cyclone seed data.
